# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

The primary user is a truck/vehicle driver working for the «Умная логистика» platform. They use the app while moving between warehouses, loading points, and delivery addresses — often outdoors, in varying light, with intermittent connectivity and limited patience for navigation friction. Their core job is to accept an order, follow the route, load and unload cargo, document each step with photos, and keep dispatch updated without paperwork.

## Product Purpose

«Умный Водитель» is the driver's working tool: it replaces phone calls, paper waybills, and scattered status updates with a single flow that shows what to do next and captures proof automatically. Success means the driver can complete a shift without leaving the app, and dispatch sees accurate status and location history in real time.

## Positioning

Connects driver, cargo, and route in one glance.

## Brand Personality

Reliable, direct, confident. The app speaks plainly, moves quickly, and never tries to entertain. It treats the driver as a skilled professional who needs the right information at the right moment, not as a user to be nudged or gamified.

## Anti-references

- Over-gamified or playful interfaces — badges, streaks, loud rewards, or cartoonish illustrations that distract from real work.
- Corporate-bureaucratic gray — dense forms, legalese, joyless data grids that feel like paperwork in disguise.
- Dark-mode cyber/terminal — neon on black, technical density, an unfriendly aesthetic that contradicts the clarity drivers need in the field.

## Design Principles

1. **Driver first, dispatcher second.** Every screen optimizes for the person doing the work, not the person reviewing it later. Status and proof are captured as a byproduct of the driver's natural flow.
2. **One glance, one action.** The most important next step is immediately visible and reachable with minimal taps. Ambiguity costs time and trust.
3. **Trust through clarity.** Route, cargo, status, and required actions are communicated explicitly. The driver should always know what is expected now and what happens next.
4. **Respect the context.** The interface works in motion, in bright sun and low light, with gloves, and on unstable connections. Touch targets, contrast, and offline behavior are first-class concerns.
5. **Proof without friction.** Photos, location, and status updates are captured as part of the workflow, not as extra homework.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.1 AA for contrast, touch target size, and focus visibility.
- Support `prefers-reduced-motion`: all animations have an instant or crossfade fallback.
- Color-blind safe: status and critical actions do not rely on color alone; icons, text, and shape reinforce meaning.
- Interface language is Russian (`Locale('ru', 'RU')`), with clear, field-appropriate labels.
